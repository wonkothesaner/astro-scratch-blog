I stopped paid employment on 25th May 2026.

I had started with Jaguar Motor Cars in 1988, the year I left school.

In the years between 1998 and 2026, I was paid by Jaguar, Quails Catering, A.W.Chapman, Marshall Specialist Vehicles, Druid (with secondment to Synergy) that was bought by FI Group, which then became Xansa, Capita, and Capgemini.

Apart from waiting on tables and cleaning dishes in a golf club, I worked in two different industry sectors - Automotive manufacturing and IT, specifically implementnig SAP.

Along the way, I collected any number of memories:

- In 1988 as part of Jaguar's training program, I was taught it's impossible to file a piece of metal perfectly flat
- At the end of 1992, I remember walking around the car park in Ford Genk counting which Ford Mondeos were missing the driver or passenger side seats. I worked for the company that supplied the seat runners and, as I came to understand was normal, we had not tooled up to support full production volumes. Unfortunately, it turned out that the Mondeo was the first car in living memory that hit full production volumes and consequently we slowly ran out of parts to supply them. Once the problem became clear and the seat-less cars started piling up in the car part, the Ford Genk site Director paid our manufacturing facility a visit. He could not believe that I, a 23-year-old, was in charge of our (admittedly small) Just-In-Time (JIT) production line and appeared to leave in a grump having told us to "pull our finger out".
